# Meta ads benchmarks for a small Israeli click-to-WhatsApp B2B campaign (researched 26 Oct 2026)

Source labels used throughout: **[VENDOR]** = an ad-tech or agency company publishing its own customer data or estimates. **[OFFICIAL]** = Meta or the Bank of Israel. **[INDEPENDENT]** = neither. No **[INDEPENDENT]** benchmark was found for any metric in this brief.

**Currency used:** USD/ILS ≈ **3.08**. This is the midpoint of the most recent dated figures found: 3.078 (representative rate, 12 Oct 2026, [Rotter](https://rotter.net/shaar/)), 3.09 (16 Oct 2026, [Calculatzia](https://www.calculatzia.com/shaar-dolar.html)) and 3.059 (Bank of Israel representative rate as quoted by [MSL](https://msl.org.il/currency/usd-to-ils/), 5 Oct 2026). These are aggregator pages, not checked against boi.org.il.
**Correction to the brief:** the brief's "75.5 ILS ≈ $20" implies about 3.78 ILS per dollar, which is an old rate. At about 3.08, Day-5 numbers convert as follows:
- CPM 75.5 ILS ≈ **$24.5**
- Cost per all-click 2.63 ILS ≈ $0.85
- Cost per conversation 141.9 ILS ≈ $46
- Cost per relevant conversation 284 ILS ≈ $92
- Target 40 ILS ≈ $13 (25 ILS ≈ $8.1)
- January 2026 result 47 ILS ≈ $15.3

All ILS figures below were converted from USD at 3.08 for comparison only. They are conversions, not the sources' own numbers.

## 1. Israel: published Meta CPM / CPC / CTR / cost-per-lead numbers

### Takeaway
The only Israel-specific numeric series found is Superads [VENDOR]. It reports monthly *medians* across "all industries" and does not split by objective, so there is no messages-objective or WhatsApp series. On it, Israel's CPM ran far below global: a 12-month median of about $9.15 (≈28 ILS), a low of $6.19 (Sep 2025), $8.13 in Oct 2025 and a peak of $15.18 in Nov 2025. Elad's Day-5 CPM of about $24.5 is about 2.7x the Israeli median and about 1.6x last November's peak.

Israeli agency blogs give only unsourced ranges: CPM 15–85 ILS and cost per lead (CPL) 40–120 ILS for professional services. No official or independent Israeli source was found: no IAB Israel, Israeli Marketing Association or Ifat report with Meta prices.

### Inferences
- **CPM 75.5 ILS (≈$24.5):**
  - About 2.7x the Israeli median ($9.15).
  - About 3x Israel's Oct 2025 median ($8.13).
  - About 1.6x Israel's Nov 2025 peak ($15.18).
  - Roughly equal to the *global* Nov 2025 peak median ($24.26, see section 5).
  - It is also inside the upper half of the agencies' unsourced 15–85 ILS range.
  - The likely drivers (inference, not measured): a small warm custom audience of 3–3.5k people, a B2B niche, and auction pressure ahead of the 27 Oct 2026 Knesset election (see section 5).
- **Cost per all-click 2.63 ILS (≈$0.85):** in line with Israel's highest monthly median CPC ($0.83, Nov 2025) and inside The Cyborg's 0.8–2.5 ILS range (at its top). The comparison is uncertain because Superads does not say whether its CPC uses link clicks or all clicks. Elad's cost per *link* click would be higher than 2.63 ILS.
- **CTR(all) 2.87%:** above the Israeli median CTR (1.66% in Oct 2025, about 1.75% on average). However, CTR(all) counts carousel swipes, "see more" and profile clicks, so it is not like-for-like with a link-based CTR. With 108 all-clicks producing 2 conversations, the click count looks inflated by non-link clicks.
- **Cost per conversation 141.9 ILS and cost per relevant conversation 284 ILS:** above The Cyborg's unsourced "professional services" CPL range (40–120 ILS) and well above Elad's own January 2026 result of about 47 ILS. These are lead ranges, not WhatsApp-conversation ranges, so the comparison is directional only.

### Gaps
- No Israeli data was found from official or independent bodies: IAB Israel, the Israeli Marketing Association (איגוד השיווק), Ifat, or the Geocartography Institute. The searches returned only old Facebook user-count articles and a 2019 Israeli Marketing Association advertiser survey with no price data.
- No Israeli data was found for link CTR, link CPC, CPM by objective (messages vs traffic vs leads), or Instagram-only figures.
- Other Israeli agency price guides appeared in search results but were not opened, so their figures are not listed here: [Sitelinx](https://sitelinx.co.il/%D7%9B%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%94-%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A7/), [tzd-seo](https://tzd-seo.org.il/facebook-prices/), [Bidernet](https://bidernet.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A8-%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%9C%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-2025/), [Simple-Web](https://www.simple-web.co.il/en/blog/facebook-instagram-ads-cost), [Aviv Malka](https://aviv-malka.com/facebook-ads-complete-guide/), [Alai](https://alai.co.il/facebook-sponsored-promotion/facebook-paid-advertising-cost-guide/). The search summary says they also give unsourced CPM ranges of roughly 15–90 ILS.
- The exchange rate was not checked against the Bank of Israel's own site.

## 2. Industry benchmarks 2024–2026 (WordStream/LocaliQ and others), by objective

### Takeaway
WordStream/LocaliQ [VENDOR] is the only source found that publishes CTR, CPC, conversion rate and CPL by industry for Meta, split by objective (traffic vs leads). Its "leads" objective explicitly includes messages, so it is the closest available stand-in for click-to-WhatsApp, though it mixes forms, calls and messages.

In the 2026 report, the lead-campaign average CPL is **$27.39 (≈84 ILS)** with a conversion rate of 8.54%. The closest industries are Home & Home Improvement ($42.95, ≈132 ILS), Furniture ($39.25, ≈121 ILS) and Personal Services ($38.09, ≈117 ILS). The report does not state its country or data period. WordStream/LocaliQ is historically US-centric, but the page does not say so.

**Superads global [VENDOR]**

### Inferences
- Elad's 141.9 ILS per conversation (≈$46) is about 1.1x LocaliQ's Home & Home Improvement CPL ($42.95) and about 1.7x the all-industry lead CPL ($27.39).
  - The relevant-conversation cost of 284 ILS (≈$92) is about 2.1x the Home & Home Improvement figure.
  - The target of 40 ILS (≈$13) is close to LocaliQ's *cheapest* lead verticals (Real Estate $13.74, Arts & Entertainment $14.59) and below every home/furniture/service vertical.
  - On LocaliQ numbers, then, the 40 ILS target is ambitious for a B2B niche. Elad's own January 2026 result (≈47 ILS ≈ $15) shows it is reachable for this advertiser.
- Elad's click-to-conversation rate is 1.9%, measured on *all* clicks. LocaliQ's lead conversion rates (5.3–15.9% in the relevant verticals) are measured per click on a mostly form-based objective.
  - The denominators differ (all-clicks vs link clicks), so the gap is smaller than it looks. It is still large.

### Gaps
- No objective-level messages benchmark was found from Varos, Triple Whale, Databox, AdAmigo or Bïrch (formerly Revealbot). These were not reached within the search budget, or did not surface in searches. Revealbot's public cost analysis found covers 2021–2023 US data only (section 5).
- WordStream/LocaliQ does not state its country, sample or period. Treat it as probably US-weighted, but this is unconfirmed.

## 3. Messages / click-to-WhatsApp specific: cost per conversation, click-to-conversation rate, reply rate

### Takeaway
There is **no independent public benchmark** for click-to-WhatsApp cost per conversation, CTR or click-to-conversation rate, by country or by industry. Two vendors that sell WhatsApp tools state this explicitly, as of May and Oct 2026.

What does exist:
1. Meta success stories, which give *relative* improvements, not absolute benchmarks.
2. One Meta test of 734k businesses on messaging destinations (relative only).
3. Unsourced vendor ranges, all consumer- or LATAM-oriented.

**No Israeli click-to-WhatsApp cost per conversation was found anywhere.**

### Cited Findings
- **No independent benchmark:**
  - Kanal [VENDOR], dated 26 May 2026 but citing Meta pages "read on October 23, 2026": "There is no independent public benchmark for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads by industry, region or placement." It gives no CTR, cost per conversation or conversion rate figures ([Kanal](https://getkanal.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks-2026)).
  - Spur [VENDOR], 1 Oct 2026, figures checked 29 Sep–1 Oct 2026: "no independent public click to WhatsApp ads benchmarks for CTR or cost per conversation" ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)).
- **Meta success stories, as compiled by Spur.** These show *relative* changes when switching optimisation from conversations/messages to leads/purchases. Spur notes that success stories only show tests that went well ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)):

  | Business | Market | Result |
  |---|---|---|
  | Confer On | Indonesia | cost per purchase −79% |
  | Thành Vinh | Malaysia | cost per purchase −40% |
  | VIP Leather Craft | Indonesia | cost per purchase −21% |
  | Braip | Brazil | cost per lead (CPL) −30% |
  | Nutryfit | India/Gulf | CPL −12% (Meta A/B test, 1–18 Apr 2025) |
  | Engelife | Brazil | cost per qualified lead −37% |
  | Learnwise | India | cost per qualified lead −31% |
  | Arabian Automobiles | UAE | CPL −56% (combined with website ads) |

  No Israeli case study was found.
- **Meta test, as reported by Spur:** across 734,000 businesses, ads with more than one message destination had a **9.5% lower median cost per message** than WhatsApp-only ads. The test ran for 3 weeks in H2 2024 and is not split by country or industry. The primary Meta page was not found in search, so this is unverified at source ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)).
- **Qualification rate, one account:** House Hunt, an Indian real-estate Spur customer running Instagram DM ads (not WhatsApp), over 14 days:
  - Cost per message start was about ₹33, then about ₹36.
  - The qualified share of chats rose from **22.1% (840/3,800) to 36.9% (1,200/3,250)**.
  - Cost per qualified lead fell from ₹150 to ₹98.

  ([Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks))
- **Unsourced vendor cost-per-conversation ranges (consumer-heavy, no methodology):**
  - Chatarmin: €1.50–8.00 per conversation, as quoted by [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks).
  - Aurora Inbox, "Meta Ads LATAM 2025": $1–3 per conversation started ([Aurora Inbox](https://www.aurorainbox.com/en/2026/05/13/what-is-click-to-whatsapp-ads/)).
  - Message Central, Brazil "2026 benchmarks": R$3–8 for fashion/beauty and R$15–40 for real estate ([Message Central](https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-brazil)).
  - Sleekflow: one skincare brand's WhatsApp conversations cost 37% less than its usual approach ([Sleekflow](https://sleekflow.io/en-us/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads)).
- **WhatsApp fees are separate from ad cost:**
  - Meta's pricing documentation (as read by Kanal on 23 Oct 2026) gives a free entry-point window of **up to 7 days** after a click-to-WhatsApp conversation starts, and 1,000 free service messages per number per month ([Kanal](https://getkanal.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks-2026)).
  - So the cost per conversation Ads Manager reports is effectively the media cost. Meta's own per-message fees only apply outside that window.
  - Israel's marketing-message rate is about $0.035–0.04 per message according to an Israeli guide ([Spike](https://www.spike.co.il/blog/whatsapp-business-pricing)). This comes from a search summary and was not opened.
- **Israeli Hebrew sources:** [Natanel Elimelech's guide](https://natanelimelech.co.il/%D7%A7%D7%9E%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%95%D7%95%D7%90%D7%98%D7%A1%D7%90%D7%A4-%D7%91%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A7/) explicitly says it has no verified conversation price. The Cyborg's WhatsApp-leads article (10 Aug 2026) has no cost per conversation ([The Cyborg](https://thecyborg.co.il/magazine/facebook-leads-to-whatsapp/)).

### Inferences
- There is no credible external number to judge "141.9 ILS per conversation" or "284 ILS per relevant conversation" against. The most defensible comparators are:
  1. The advertiser's own January 2026 result (about 47 ILS per conversation).
  2. LocaliQ lead-objective CPLs, which include messages (section 2).
  3. The owner's own target of up to 40 ILS per relevant conversation.
- House Hunt's 22–37% qualified share is one Indian consumer account, but it is the only published "relevant out of all conversations" ratio found. Elad's 1 of 2 (50%) is too small a sample to compare.
- Meta's success stories suggest that, *for consumer advertisers with volume*, optimising for leads or a downstream event rather than conversations often cuts cost per qualified result. That requires conversion signals, such as the Conversions API for Business Messaging, which a campaign with 2 conversations cannot feed yet.

### Gaps
- **No Israeli click-to-WhatsApp cost-per-conversation benchmark exists** in any source found, whether Hebrew or English, official or vendor.
- **No click-to-conversation rate benchmark** (clicks to started WhatsApp chats) was found anywhere, for any country.
- **No reply-rate benchmark** for click-to-WhatsApp chats was found. The "45% response rate vs 6% email" claim on an Israeli blog is about WhatsApp broadcasts, has no source, and is not about ads ([Yahav Rubin](https://yahavrubin.com/whatsapp-marketing/)).
- One search summary mentioned "about 22 ILS average cost per conversation" from an Israeli case study. It could not be traced to a source page, so it is **unverified and should not be used**.
- No Meta Israel success story for click-to-WhatsApp or messaging ads was found.

## 4. B2B on Meta, warm (retargeting) vs cold audiences, and frequency norms

### Takeaway
The only B2B-specific Meta dataset found is Metadata.io [VENDOR], covering 153 B2B advertisers in 2025. It puts Facebook CPL at **$145 (≈447 ILS)** and Instagram at **$138 (≈425 ILS)**, with B2B paid-social CTRs well under 1%. These are form leads from B2B software-style advertisers, so they are a ceiling-type reference rather than a match for a local photographer.

Vendor guidance consistently says warm audiences show higher CTR *and* higher CPM than cold ones. Frequency norms are vendor rules of thumb only: roughly 1.5–3 per week for cold audiences and roughly 3–8 per week for small warm audiences. There is no Meta-official frequency benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- **Metadata.io 2026 B2B benchmark report (2025 data) [VENDOR]** ([Metadata report](https://metadata.io/md/benchmark-report-2026.md)):
  - Sample: 153 B2B advertisers across LinkedIn, Google, Facebook and Instagram. No per-channel sample size on the fetched page.
  - CPL: Facebook **$145**, Instagram **$138**.
  - Cross-channel: retargeting **$196/lead** vs cold prospecting **$187/lead**. These are not stated to be Meta-specific.
- **Metadata.io B2B benchmarks page** (from a search-result extract; **not confirmed** on the fetched report page) ([Metadata B2B benchmarks](https://metadata.io/b2b-advertising-benchmarks)):
  - Facebook: CTR **0.79%**, CPC **$1.95**, click-to-lead **2.6%**, n = 71 advertisers.
  - Also from Metadata: 0.65–0.80% is a "good" CTR for B2B paid social, and the average Facebook CTR in 2024 was 0.77% ([Metadata blog](https://metadata.io/resources/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks/)).
- **Warm vs cold [VENDOR, no methodology]:**
  - AdAdvisor: prospecting CTR 0.8–1.5% vs retargeting 2–5%; prospecting CPM $6–15 vs retargeting $10–25 ([AdAdvisor](https://adadvisor.ai/docs/learn/retargeting)).
  - Webtonic (automotive): cold CTR 0.6–1.4% vs 30-day website-retargeting 1.4–3.2%; cold CPM $15–24 vs warm $22–32 ([Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/automotive-facebook-ads-statistics)).
- **Frequency [VENDOR rules of thumb]:**
  - Cold prospecting: about 1.5–3 per week, with fatigue flagged above about 3–4 ([AdAdvisor](https://adadvisor.ai/blog/frequency-cap-facebook); [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-ads-frequency-management)).
  - Remarketing audiences: about 6–8 before fatigue ([Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/how-to-spot-meta-ad-fatigue/)).
  - Fatigue signal: frequency rising while CTR drops ≥20% ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-ads-frequency-management)).

### Inferences
- **CPM:** a warm custom audience of about 3,000–3,500 people is exactly the small, high-intent kind of audience the vendor tables price *higher* than cold audiences. Part of the gap between the 75.5 ILS CPM and the Israeli all-industry median (about 28 ILS) is structural, not a sign of a broken campaign. The part that is structural is not quantified.
- **CTR and cost:** Elad's CTR(all) of 2.87% is within the vendor "retargeting" CTR range (2–5%) and far above B2B paid-social norms (0.65–0.80%). Cost per relevant conversation (284 ILS) is still below Metadata's B2B Facebook CPL ($145 ≈ 447 ILS). The two are not like-for-like: Metadata's figures are form leads for B2B software-style advertisers.
- **Frequency:** a warm pool of about 3k will build frequency fast at 75 ILS/day. By the vendor rules above, about 6–8 per week is the point to watch CTR, not a hard cap.

### Gaps
- No Meta B2B benchmark specific to professional or creative services (photographers, architects, interior designers) was found, in Israel or anywhere else.
- No Meta-official benchmark for warm vs cold CPM or CTR, and no official frequency benchmark, was found.
- Metadata's Facebook CTR, CPC and click-to-lead figures (n = 71) could not be confirmed on the fetched page.

## 5. Seasonality: Q4/November CPM rise (Israel and global), Israeli holidays, October 2026

### Takeaway
Both the Israeli and global series show a sharp November spike.
- **Israel:** Superads' median CPM almost doubled from **$8.13 in Oct 2025 to $15.18 in Nov 2025** (+87%), then fell to $10.71 in December.
- **Global:** the median rose +20.7% into November 2025, to a $24.26 peak.
- **Historical US data:** Revealbot's 2021–2023 analysis showed costs spiking from Black Friday, peaking about 40% above the year average and dipping about 20% below it in January–February.

No data was found on how Israeli holidays (Sukkot) affect Meta CPM. **27 Oct 2026 is Knesset election day.** No data quantifies the election's effect on Israeli Meta auctions.

### Cited Findings

## 6. Facebook feed vs Instagram feed CTR

### Takeaway
Every source found shows Facebook Feed CTR well above Instagram Feed CTR, with similar CPMs. The cleanest recent data, Emplifi Q2 2026 (worldwide, entertainment vertical), has FB Feed CTR at **2.30% vs IG Feed 0.45%**, about 5x. Elad's FB 3.87% vs IG 1.43% (about 2.7x) follows the same direction.

### Cited Findings
- **Emplifi, Q2 2026 (1 Apr–30 Jun 2026), worldwide, entertainment vertical** [VENDOR]. CTR definition and paid sample size are not stated ([Emplifi](https://emplifi.io/?p=29280)):

  | Placement | CTR | CPC | CPM |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | FB Feed | 2.30% | ~$0.30 | ~$3.10 |
  | IG Feed | 0.45% | ~$0.65 | ~$3.40 |
  | FB Reels | 1.15% | | |
  | IG Reels | 0.50% | | |
  | IG Stories | 0.50% | | |

- **Emplifi, Q3 2025, all brands** (from a search-result extract; the source page was not opened): FB Feed CTR 2.23%, Reels CTR 1.3%, and CPC lowest on FB Feed and Reels (under $0.50) ([Agility PR / Emplifi](https://www.agilitypr.com/?p=98867)).
- **Israel:** ActiveLead [VENDOR, unsourced] says Instagram is "usually 10–20%" more expensive than Facebook in Israel ([ActiveLead](https://www.activelead.co.il/blog/facebook-ads-cost-israel-2026)).

### Inferences
- Elad's FB Feed CTR(all) 3.87% vs IG Feed 1.43% is the normal direction. The FB-to-IG gap (about 2.7x) is smaller than in Emplifi's entertainment data (about 5x).
- The CTR gap alone does not show which placement produces conversations more cheaply. That needs placement-level cost per conversation, which is in the project's own data, not in public benchmarks.

### Gaps

- No B2B or professional-services placement-level data was found.
- No data was found on carousel-format CTR by placement for click-to-WhatsApp, or for any objective, from a credible source.
