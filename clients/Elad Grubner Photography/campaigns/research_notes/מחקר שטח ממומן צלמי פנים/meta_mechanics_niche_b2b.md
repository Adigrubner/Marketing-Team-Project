# Meta Ads Mechanics for a Niche B2B Service in Israel (Small Budget, WhatsApp Goal) - 2025-2026

Scope: platform mechanics and benchmarks that decide whether a proven creative idea can work for Elad Grubner (interior/architecture photographer; audience = a few thousand Israeli interior designers and architects; about 50 ILS/day, hard cap 100 ILS/day per ad set; goal = WhatsApp conversations from designers; target cost per *relevant* conversation up to 40 ILS per the client brief).
Research date: 15 Oct 2026. Notes on source quality are inline. "Vendor" = a company that sells WhatsApp or ads tools, so its numbers are marketing claims.
Method note: Meta Business Help Center pages could not be read by the fetch tool (they render with JavaScript and only the page title came back). Official Meta facts below therefore come from Meta for Developers docs, Meta-attributed figures quoted in trade press, and Jon Loomer (an independent Meta-ads specialist whose site cites Meta docs). Anything that rests only on a help-center page I could not read is marked.

## 1. Targeting in 2025-2026: what is left, and how small B2B niches are reached now

### Takeaway
Meta has spent 2022-2026 removing and merging detailed-targeting options, removed detailed-targeting *exclusions* in 2025, and made Advantage+ Audience the default. Under it, interests, lookalikes and custom audiences are only *suggestions*; the hard limits are location, minimum age, language and custom-audience exclusions. No source shows a reliable way to target "interior designers in Israel" by job title. The realistic options are: (a) broad/Advantage+ targeting in Israel where the creative itself picks out designers ("creative as targeting"), and (b) retargeting Elad's own engagers, video viewers and site visitors, or a customer list, with expansion turned off.

### Cited Findings
**Removals and consolidation timeline**
- 19 Jan 2022: Meta removed "sensitive" detailed-targeting options (health causes, sexual orientation, religious practices, political affiliation, race/ethnicity). 15 Jan 2024: further consolidation, with existing ad sets given until 18 Mar 2024 to update. 23 Jun 2025: granular interests were merged into broader groups. 15 Jan 2026: final deadline, after which ad sets still using the affected interests stopped delivering. Detailed-targeting exclusions were also removed in 2025, while custom-audience exclusions remain. — [LinearDesign, "Facebook Detailed Targeting in 2026", updated 3 Jul 2026](https://lineardesign.com/blog/facebook-detailed-targeting-list-2024/)
- Aug 2025: Meta posted a notice in Ads Manager that read "Some detailed targeting options have been combined" and "Starting Jan 15, 2026, ad sets using the unavailable options will stop delivering." Neither article lists the specific options removed. — [MediaPost, 18 Aug 2025](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/408262/meta-further-consolidates-ad-targeting-options.); [Social Media Today, 17 Aug 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-removes-more-detailed-ad-targeting-options-facebook-instagram/757856/)
- Meta's stated reasons: the options were rarely used, too granular, redundant, or touched topics people may see as sensitive. The broader aim is to cut back manual targeting, which Meta says often limits performance. Meta also said that in its own testing, removing detailed-targeting exclusions improved median cost per conversion by 22.6% (a Meta-reported figure). — [Social Media Today, 17 Aug 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-removes-more-detailed-ad-targeting-options-facebook-instagram/757856/)
- Detailed-targeting exclusions were taken away for new campaigns. Exclusions by custom audience and account-level audience controls are still available. — [Social Media Today (search result)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-removes-detailed-targeting-exclusions-from-ad-campaigns/723389/)

**Job title / employer / industry targeting**
- History: in Sep 2017 Facebook removed targeting on the self-reported fields employer, job title, school and field of study, after ProPublica's "Jew Haters" report. About 5,000 common terms (for example "nurse", "teacher") were later restored after manual review. This is old background. — [Social Media Today, 2017](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/social-networks/facebook-removes-major-targeting-options-fight-discriminatory-advertising); [MarTech](https://martech.org/facebooks-ad-targeting-options-removal-not-apply-existing-campaigns/)
- As of mid-2026, a third-party guide still lists "Job title and industry (professional demographics including employer)" as a demographic category and calls manual job-title/industry targeting suitable for B2B. It also recommends custom audiences built from first-party data as the main B2B targeting tool. — [LinearDesign, updated Jul 2026](https://lineardesign.com/blog/facebook-detailed-targeting-list-2024/)
- Practitioners describe job-title and seniority data as unreliable, since it is self-reported (and partly from third-party data in the past). The B2B "workarounds" they suggest are custom audiences and lookalikes from CRM lists. — [AIMClear (older)](https://aimclear.com/facebook-job-title-debacle-alternate-targeting-workaround-hacks/); [Jon Loomer, B2B targeting (date not verified)](https://www.jonloomer.com/b2b-targeting-facebook-ads/)

**Advantage+ Audience behavior**
- Advantage+ Audience is now the default. Custom audiences, lookalikes and detailed targeting entered there are treated as suggestions, and Meta can deliver beyond them. — [Jon Loomer Pubcast, 19 Feb 2025](https://pubcast.jonloomer.com/when-to-use-advantage-audience/)
- Hard "Audience Controls" under Advantage+ Audience are: location, minimum age, excluded custom audiences, and language (language only where it is uncommon for the location). Maximum age and gender are suggestions only. Without a suggestion, Meta builds the audience from past conversions, pixel data and earlier ad interactions. Loomer notes that this behaves much like remarketing before Meta expands further. — [Jon Loomer, Advantage+ Audience Best Practices, mod. 6 Mar 2025](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-audience-best-practices-guide/)
- Loomer suggests adding audience suggestions when the account or pixel has little history, or when the business serves several distinct customer groups and wants Meta to focus on one. For lead generation, he says to watch lead quality and switch back to "original audiences" if leads are poor. With Advantage+ Audience, one ad set per campaign is usually enough, because several cold ad sets overlap in auctions, which raises costs and can lengthen learning. — [Jon Loomer, Advantage+ Audience Best Practices](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-audience-best-practices-guide/)
- Meta's documentation (quoted by Loomer) claims Advantage+ Audience gives: 33% lower cost per result (Meta test, Mar-Jun 2023), 13% lower median cost per catalog sale, 7% lower median cost per website conversion, and 28% lower average cost per click/lead/landing-page view. Loomer warns the last figure can be a red flag, because cheaper low-funnel actions are not always better ones. These are Meta claims. — [Jon Loomer, Advantage+ Audience Best Practices](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-audience-best-practices-guide/)
- You can still switch to "original audiences" through a link at the bottom of Advantage+ Audience, after a warning. There, age, gender, location and language are hard limits, and custom-audience expansion can be turned off. Under the newer "Advantage+ campaign setup", a custom audience is a suggestion by default, but unticking the box makes it a hard limit. So pure remarketing to a custom audience is still possible. — [Jon Loomer (search summary of jonloomer.com posts 2024-2025)](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-campaign-setup-targeting/)
- Loomer's own test of Advantage+ Audience against detailed targeting and lookalikes found no meaningful evidence that manual restrictions improved results. His advice is to put the effort into creative. Note that I saw this only through an aggregator summary. — [echai.ventures summary of Jon Loomer](https://echai.ventures/d2c/from/jon-loomer-digital)

**Custom audiences and lookalikes**
- A lookalike source needs at least 100 people from one country. Meta recommends a source of 1,000-5,000. In small countries Loomer suggests raising the lookalike percentage to reach enough people (for comparison, 1% of the US is about 2.8M people). He thinks lookalikes probably now beat interests, because interests keep being removed, but says to test them against fully broad targeting. Advantage Lookalike, which can widen the percentage, is on by default and cannot always be turned off. — [Jon Loomer, Lookalike Audiences guide, updated 1 Mar 2025](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-ads-lookalike-audiences/)
- People who engaged with your Facebook Page or Instagram profile are a valid custom-audience source, and therefore a valid lookalike seed. — [JetFuel guide (search result)](https://jetfuel.agency/meta-lookalike-audiences-guide/)

**Small-audience problems (frequency)**
- For small B2B pools with long sales cycles, one B2B ads vendor suggests starting frequency caps at about 1-2 impressions per person per week for prospecting and 3-5 per week for retargeting, and raising them only when the data supports it. Rotating more creative variations lets a small pool tolerate more frequency. This is vendor advice, not Meta guidance. — [Flighted](https://www.flighted.co/blog/frequency-capping-best-practices-b2b-saas-meta-advertisers)
- B2B retargeting on Meta can look most efficient in the dashboard (cheaper CPMs, reported conversions) at exactly the point where the same small group is being hit over and over. Judge it on real pipeline, not on platform-reported results. — [Directive Consulting](https://directiveconsulting.com/blog/instagram-retargeting-ads-b2b-guide/)

### Inferences
- There is no evidence that Meta can isolate "Israeli interior designers" through targeting settings. Interests such as "Interior design" are consumer interests (homeowners, renovators), so an interest-based ad set will mostly reach homeowners. The filter that does the real work is the creative: a headline and visual that speak only to designers, such as "for designers with a finished project", backstage shots, or credit to the designer. This matches the "creative as targeting" approach Loomer and Meta push.
- The most reliable way to reach actual designers is first-party: people who engaged with Elad's Instagram/Facebook (his organic strategy targets designers), his video viewers, his site visitors, and a customer list of past designer clients. That list could also seed an Israel lookalike, but a seed of a few hundred is near the minimum and will be noisy.
- Because detailed-targeting exclusions are gone, Elad cannot exclude a consumer interest such as "homeowners". He can still exclude custom audiences, for example past clients or people who already messaged.
- Practical structure: 1 campaign, 1 main ad set (Advantage+ Audience, Israel, minimum age about 25, with Elad's engagers and customer list as suggestions). An optional small, separate retargeting ad set (custom audience only, expansion off) makes sense only if its frequency stays reasonable. See section 4.

### Gaps
- I found no source confirming whether "Job title / Employer / Industry" demographic targeting exists in Israel today, or how large an "interior designer" job-title audience would be. Only a check in Ads Manager can answer this (see the audience-size estimate in the Ads Manager UI).
- I could not find which specific interests were merged or removed in June 2025 and January 2026, or whether "Interior design" or "Architecture" interests survived. No source lists them.
- The exact retention windows for Instagram engagers and video viewers (commonly up to 365 days) and the current minimum custom-audience size for delivery were not verified from an official page in this session (Meta Help Center would not render).

## 2. Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) ads: setup, objectives, counting, benchmarks, lead quality

### Takeaway
A CTWA ad needs a Facebook Page with a linked WhatsApp number. It can run under the Engagement, Leads, Sales or Traffic objective, and for WhatsApp the default performance goal is "maximize number of conversations". It supports a custom greeting and a pre-filled customer message plus FAQ buttons. Meta also offers a lead-optimized goal ("Maximize number of leads through messaging"), but it requires at least 10 lead or purchase events in 30 days, shared through the WhatsApp Business app or the Conversions API, and is rolling out gradually. No credible Israel-specific or B2B cost-per-conversation benchmark exists. The vendor numbers available (roughly $0.5-$5 per conversation) are consumer-oriented and say nothing about cost per *relevant designer* conversation.

### Cited Findings
**Setup and objectives (official)**
- Supported objectives for CTWA are OUTCOME_ENGAGEMENT, OUTCOME_LEADS, OUTCOME_SALES and OUTCOME_TRAFFIC. The optimization goals are: Engagement = Conversations or Link clicks; Leads = Conversations; Sales = Conversations, Offsite conversions and others; Traffic = Conversations, Landing page views and others. The ad set needs a Facebook Page with a WhatsApp number linked. Supported formats are image, video, carousel and slideshow. Placements are Facebook, Instagram and Messenger, with WhatsApp Status available via placements. The doc references API v25.0. — [Meta for Developers, Ads that Click to WhatsApp](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/messaging-ads/click-to-whatsapp)
- The greeting can be customized (`page_welcome_message`). Meta's default pre-filled customer message is "Hello! Can I get more info on this?". The ad can also use autofill messages, greeting text, icebreakers (FAQ buttons), and automated greeting buttons (Call, View Website, View Catalog, or a WhatsApp Flow form). — [Meta for Developers, Ads that Click to WhatsApp](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/messaging-ads/click-to-whatsapp)
- Meta's WhatsApp Business site says CTWA ads can be created in Ads Manager *or from the Facebook Page*, run on Facebook and Instagram (Feed, Stories, Marketplace), and use location, age, gender, custom or lookalike targeting. Measurement beyond the conversation goes through the pixel, Conversions API or offline conversions. — [WhatsApp Business, Ads that click to WhatsApp (© 2026)](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)
- On Instagram, a WhatsApp ad can be made by boosting a post: choose the "More messages" goal, then pick WhatsApp. This requires adding the WhatsApp business number to the Instagram profile's contact options. The feature launched in 2021 and Instagram said availability was rolling out by country. — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/android-app/663506574978989); [Instagram for Business blog, 28 Sep 2021](https://business.instagram.com/blog/instagram-boost-ads-whatsapp-find-customers)

**Lead-optimized goal and the WhatsApp Business app (third-party guide, updated 8 Oct 2026)**
- Under the Leads objective with WhatsApp, the default goal is "Maximize number of conversations". A newer goal, "Maximize number of leads through messaging", is rolling out gradually. For CTWA it needs at least 10 lead or purchase events shared with Meta within 30 days, *through the WhatsApp Business app or the Conversions API*. Engagement and Traffic are described as simpler, older options. Sales with WhatsApp-only is described as not supported (it needs several messaging destinations). — [SendPulse knowledge base, updated 8 Oct 2026](https://sendpulse.com/knowledge-base/chatbot/whatsapp/create-whatsapp-ad) (vendor guide; note that its own setup assumes the API, while it names the app as one way to share lead events)
- Meta's Conversions API for Business Messaging covers WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram Direct, with events such as LeadSubmitted, QualifiedLead and Purchase. Purchase optimization is available for click-to-WhatsApp and click-to-Messenger ads. For WhatsApp it needs the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) plus a developer app. — [Meta for Developers, Conversions API for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/business-messaging)
- The free WhatsApp Business app is described as enough for businesses handling fewer than about 50-100 messages a day that reply by hand, and it connects to a Facebook Page through Business Settings. The API (through a Meta-approved provider, after business verification) is needed for automation. — [Benly.ai guide, 24 Mar 2026](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/click-to-whatsapp-messenger-ads) (vendor-style guide; it also refers to a "Messages objective" that no longer exists in Ads Manager, so treat it with caution)

**How conversations are counted**
- "Messaging conversations started" counts when a person sends their first message to the business after interacting with the ad. Some guides add that repeat messages from the same person within a 7-day window count as one conversation, so the number of conversations is lower than the number of messages. — [Benly.ai](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/click-to-whatsapp-messenger-ads) (first-message definition); the 7-day rule appeared only in secondary search summaries, see Gaps.
- One CTWA guide warns that a click is not a lead: the person still has to send the message and the business still has to reply. It recommends measuring real inbound messages and qualified outcomes, not chat launches. — [360dialog best practices](https://www.360dialog.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-best-practices) (vendor)

**WhatsApp message fees (relevant only if Elad moves to the API)**
- From 1 Jul 2025 Meta charges per message on the WhatsApp Business *Platform*. Only delivered template messages are charged, and free-form replies are free inside the 24-hour service window. When a user messages through a CTWA ad from the Android or iOS app (not desktop or web), the business's reply within 24 hours opens a 72-hour "free entry point" window in which every message is free. Israel has its own rate line (code 972). — [Meta for Developers, WhatsApp Business Platform pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)
- Conflict: a Times of Israel blog post and a vendor say that from 1 Oct 2026, service replies are charged (Israel about $0.0053 per message) after 1,000 free service messages per number per month. The Meta pricing page I fetched did not mention this. A Hebrew news item says the change applies only to the API, not to the regular Business app. — [Times of Israel blog](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/your-whatsapp-bots-october-bill-is-₪0-almost/); [Kanal, 26 May 2026](https://getkanal.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks-2026); [hon.co.il (search result)](https://www.hon.co.il/?p=280241)

**Benchmarks (all vendor or unsourced, none from Israel)**
- Kanal (a WhatsApp vendor, May 2026) says outright: "There is no independent public benchmark for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads by industry, region or placement." — [Kanal](https://getkanal.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks-2026). Conflict: a Hebrew search summary attributed median €0.80-1.50 per conversation to Kanal (possibly another language version). The English page I fetched contains no such figures, so do not rely on them.
- Other vendor ranges: cost per conversation started $0.50-$5.00, qualification rate 30-50%, cost per qualified lead $2-$15. None of these cite a source. — [Benly.ai](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/click-to-whatsapp-messenger-ads). Also LATAM $1-3 per conversation against $5-15 for web forms ("Meta Ads LATAM 2025 data", vendor-cited) — [Aurora Inbox (search result)](https://www.aurorainbox.com/en/2026/05/13/what-is-click-to-whatsapp-ads/); Europe €1.50-€8 (Chatarmin, unsourced) — [Chatarmin (search result)](https://chatarmin.com/blog/click-to-chat); Brazil real estate R$15-40 per conversation — [Message Central](https://www.messagecentral.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-brasil).
- Meta-published case (UAE Nissan dealer, CTWA plus website ads): 2x more leads and 56% lower cost per lead, self-reported. — via [search summary of whatsappbusiness.com case](https://whatsappbusiness.com/?p=5183)

**Quality problems and how advertisers filter**
- Vendors agree that the pre-filled message is a qualification tool. A specific opener that names the service and the person's situation gives intent signals, and each ad set or audience should get its own pre-filled message. Users can edit or delete it, though, so it cannot enforce qualification alone. Real filtering happens in the first reply: 2-3 short questions (need, timing, budget) or tappable options, plus a fast response, with under 5 minutes recommended. — [d7networks](https://d7networks.com/blog/how-to-run-click-to-whatsapp-ads/); [neorev](https://neorev.it.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads/); [360dialog](https://www.360dialog.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-best-practices) (all vendors)
- CTWA is said to produce fewer but higher-intent leads than Meta lead forms. This is a vendor claim. — [stackmatix (search result)](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-ads-whatsapp-lead-generation)

### Inferences
- The core risk for Elad: the "maximize conversations" goal teaches Meta to find *anyone* likely to start a chat. In a broad Israeli audience, that means mostly homeowners, people looking for a designer, other photographers, and job seekers. So total conversations can look cheap while relevant designer conversations stay rare. The KPI should be cost per *relevant* conversation (the brief's 25-40 ILS), counted by hand.
- Countermeasures that need no API: (1) creative that addresses designers only; (2) a designer-specific pre-filled message, e.g. "Hi Elad, I'm an interior designer with a finished project for a shoot"; (3) 2-3 FAQ/icebreaker buttons that self-sort, such as "I'm a designer with a finished project" / "I'm an architect" / "Just asking"; (4) Elad labels each chat in the WhatsApp Business app (relevant / not relevant). If "Maximize leads through messaging" becomes available in his account, those lead labels (10+ in 30 days) could later move optimization from "conversations" to "leads". Its availability in Israel is unverified.
- Elad most likely uses the free WhatsApp Business app, so the per-message API fees and the 1 Oct 2026 service-message change most likely do not apply to him. Ad spend is the only cost.
- Engagement or Leads objective: both default to "maximize conversations" for WhatsApp, so the choice matters only if the lead-optimized goal appears in the account.

### Gaps
- I found no Israeli agency or Meta data on CTWA cost per conversation, in ILS or otherwise, and no B2B or professional-services CTWA benchmark from any credible source.
- Meta's official wording of "Messaging conversations started" (including the 7-day inactivity rule) could not be read, because the Help Center did not render. Only secondary descriptions are available.
- It is not confirmed in an official doc whether "Maximize number of leads through messaging" is available to Israeli accounts using only the WhatsApp Business app.

## 3. Alternatives and trade-offs for B2B lead quality

### Takeaway
Each alternative trades volume for quality differently. Instant forms with a "Higher intent" review step and qualifying questions filter better than raw chats, but they move the conversation off WhatsApp. Retargeting engagers and video viewers reaches people who already know Elad, but small pools saturate. Boosting a proven post is simple and can send people to WhatsApp, but gives less control. Partnership ads are the most interesting fit for Elad: Meta reports lower CPA and higher CTR on average, the format supports click-to-message destinations, and the designer partner must grant permission through an ad code or account-level access. Whether WhatsApp specifically is a supported destination for partnership ads is not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
**Instant forms**
- Agency guides recommend the "Higher intent" form type (it adds a review and confirm step), qualifying questions (budget, timeline, need), conditional logic, and SMS or work-email verification. They accept that volume will drop. — [Straight North](https://www.straightnorth.com/blog/lead-generation-on-meta-9-proven-tactics-for-delivering-high-quality-leads/); [OnePPC](https://oneppcagency.co.uk/facebook-ads/lead-ads/) (agencies)
- Optimizing for lead *quality* ("Conversion leads") requires sending CRM stages back through the Conversions API for CRM. A Meta-attributed figure says connected-CRM campaigns optimizing for conversion leads averaged 15% lower cost per quality lead (quoted second-hand by an agency). — [Click Here Digital](https://www.clickheredigital.com/insights/meta-shift-to-lead-optimization)
- Agencies disagree on forms against landing pages. Instant forms suit simple enquiries where low friction matters, and a common hybrid is forms for the top of the funnel and website or booking forms for high-intent consultations. — [OnePPC](https://oneppcagency.co.uk/facebook-ads/facebook-metas-leads-ads-optimisation-lead-quantity-vs-quality/)

**Boosting posts compared with Ads Manager**
- Boosting is simpler. Ads Manager has more objectives, segmentation, retargeting and tracking. Boosting is described as enough when the desired action is simple and happens on Instagram. An Instagram boost can send people to WhatsApp through the "More messages" goal. — [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/uk/blog/how-to-boost-instagram-post); [Kawan Elite (search result)](https://kawanelite.com/?p=7375); [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/android-app/663506574978989)

**Partnership ads (running an ad from both Elad's and a designer's handles)**
- Partnership ads let an advertiser run ads together with creators, brands or other businesses. Both accounts appear in the ad header, and Meta uses signals from both accounts for ranking. The partner needs a professional Instagram account and must meet eligibility requirements. Permission is given per post (by the creator) or per account. Ads can be made by boosting existing media or creating new media. As of 25 Mar 2025, partnership ads can use click-to-message destinations (the API example uses Messenger; WhatsApp is not named). — [Meta for Developers blog, 25 Mar 2025](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2025/03/25/instagram-partnership-ads/)
- Meta's supported-configurations list for partnership ads includes: boosting existing Instagram posts, click-to-message destinations, lead generation (instant forms), testimonials, and catalog ads. — [Meta for Developers, Partnership ads supported configurations](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/partnership-ads/ads-creation/supported-configurations)
- Meta-reported performance (2025 expansion): partnership ads delivered on average 19% lower CPA and 13% higher CTR than standard formats. Some coverage says 53% CTR, which is probably a misreport. — [eMarketer (via search summary; page returned 403)](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-expands-partnership-ads-turn-creator-content-performance); [Media-Marketing (search result)](https://www.media-marketing.com/en/news/meta-builds-the-next-phase-of-advertising-through-ai-and-content-creators/)
- Content-level permission works through a partnership ad code that the creator generates in the app and sends to the brand. Since Dec 2025, creators can share codes proactively even when the brand is not tagged. Permissions can carry an expiry date (30 or 90 days, for example). One trade outlet's claim that partnership ads are *mandatory* for all creator content could not be traced to any Meta policy and should be treated as unverified. — [Cohley](https://www.cohley.com/blog/meta-partnership-ads-requirements); [Content Grip](https://www.contentgrip.com/meta-branded-content-rules-update/); [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/partnership-ads/) (secondary)
- Vendor case studies (for example, a 20% lower CPA for one brand) are anecdotes from interested parties. — [Influee](https://influee.co/it/content-hub/babylovegrow-case-study)

**Retargeting engagers and video viewers**
- If the budget is too small for prospecting, Loomer suggests spending it only on remarketing. He warns that results will look inflated and partly non-incremental, but some results are better than none. — [Jon Loomer Pubcast, 30 Apr 2025](https://pubcast.jonloomer.com/when-your-budget-is-just-too-small/)
- In small B2B retargeting pools, rotate several angles (case study, objection, process, direct offer) and cap frequency. — [Flighted](https://www.flighted.co/blog/frequency-capping-best-practices-b2b-saas-meta-advertisers) (vendor)

### Inferences
- Partnership ads fit Elad's world unusually well, because designers already tag him in collabs. An ad shown under "[Designer] and Elad Grubner" is social proof *from a peer*, which is exactly what other designers trust. The designer has to agree and send a code. One caveat: the designer's own followers are mostly potential *homeowner* clients, not designers. The ranking boost from the partner's signals may therefore pull delivery toward homeowners, so the creative must still be addressed to designers. The brief's existing publication-approval rule (a project's approval also covers ads) should still be confirmed with the specific designer for her *handle* appearing in a paid ad.
- Boosting a proven organic post or reel to WhatsApp ("More messages") is the lowest-effort test, but it skips Ads Manager controls such as custom-audience exclusions and multiple ads per ad set. For a structured test, recreating the same post in Ads Manager using the existing post ID keeps its social proof (likes and comments) and adds control.
- Instant forms with 2-3 qualifying questions ("Are you an interior designer or architect?", "Is the project finished?", "Area?") are the strongest *quality filter*, but they break the "WhatsApp conversation" goal. They could be a fallback test if CTWA brings too many irrelevant chats.

### Gaps
- No source confirms that WhatsApp (as opposed to Messenger or Instagram Direct) is a supported destination for partnership ads, or which objectives partnership ads support with a messaging destination.
- No B2B-specific data compares instant forms, CTWA and Instagram DM ads on lead quality.
- I found nothing specific on Instagram Direct (click-to-Instagram) ads for B2B; the Conversions API doc notes that purchase optimization is *not* available for Instagram Direct ads.

## 4. Budget realism at about 50 ILS/day

### Takeaway
Meta's documented guidance is about 50 optimization events in 7 days to leave the learning phase. At 50 ILS/day (350 ILS/week), that would need a cost per conversation of about 7 ILS, which is unrealistic for a niche B2B audience. The ad set will most likely stay "Learning limited". Loomer and others say this is not fatal. What follows from it: one campaign, one ad set, few ads, no splitting, few mid-test changes, and judging results only after enough spend.

### Cited Findings
- Meta guidance (quoted by PPC Land from the Business Help Center): an ad set generally needs about 50 optimization events within 7 days of its last significant edit to leave learning. Unconfirmed reports describe lower thresholds in some accounts: Loomer saw 10 events over about 3 days, Mobvista claims 25. None is officially documented. — [ppc.land, learning phase](https://ppc.land/learning-phase/); [Jon Loomer, "Is the Learning Phase Changing?"](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/is-the-learning-phase-changing/); [Mobvista](https://xmp-stats-cn.mobvista.com/en-blog/docs/How-to-benefit-from-meta-simplifying-ad-campaigns-lower-learning-phase-requirements-ahead)
- (Meta Help Center page "About the learning phase" exists at [facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700); its body could not be fetched.)
- Loomer (15 Oct 2025): "Learning" is largely a label, since the algorithm is always learning. Leaving it is better but not leaving it is not fatal, and 50 (or 25) per week may simply not be realistic. Significant edits (new or changed ads, targeting changes, big budget jumps) can restart learning, though in his test adding an ad to a well-performing ad set did not restart it. Don't avoid needed changes, and don't tinker constantly. — [Jon Loomer Pubcast, 15 Oct 2025](https://pubcast.jonloomer.com/the-learning-phase-is-just-a-label/)
- Loomer (30 Apr 2025): use about 50 conversions a week as a rule of thumb, and at least half that if possible. Budget *per ad set* is what matters, so split $1,000/day over 50 ad sets and each behaves like $20/day. Consolidate into as few ad sets as possible, ideally one. Don't draw conclusions from small differences such as 3 vs 0 or 2 vs 4. Don't switch off ads or placements on small samples. Optimize for leads that matter, not cheap ones. If the budget is still too small, consider remarketing only. — [Jon Loomer Pubcast, 30 Apr 2025](https://pubcast.jonloomer.com/when-your-budget-is-just-too-small/)
- Small budgets struggle because the required events must land inside a rolling 7-day window, so with low daily spend early events "age out" before enough build up. — [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/ad-learning-phase-small-budgets) (agency)
- Several cold ad sets under Advantage+ Audience overlap in auctions, raise costs and can lengthen learning, so one ad set per campaign is usually enough. — [Jon Loomer, Advantage+ Audience Best Practices](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-audience-best-practices-guide/)
- Israel CPM, median across campaigns, Oct 2025-Sep 2026: about $9.15, ranging from $6.19 (Sep 2025) to $15.18 (Nov 2025), about 56% below the global median of about $20.93. Month-to-month swings in Israel are larger than global. This comes from one vendor dataset (SuperAds; "$3B of ad data", directional only, with internally inconsistent period labels). An earlier window (Nov 2024-Nov 2025) showed an average of about 7.7, with Q1 2025 the most expensive quarter and Q2 2025 the cheapest. — [SuperAds Israel CPM](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/israel)

### Inferences
(These are calculations, not sourced facts. The exchange rate is an assumption: about 3.6-3.7 ILS per USD; please verify.)
- Spend: 50 ILS/day = 350 ILS/week, about 1,500 ILS/month. At the brief's thresholds, that buys about 9 relevant conversations a week at 40 ILS each, or 14 at 25 ILS. Learning-phase exit would need about 50 conversations a week (≤7 ILS each), which is not realistic, so plan to live with "Learning limited".
- Impressions: at a median CPM of about $9 (about 33 ILS), 50 ILS/day buys roughly 1,500 impressions a day, about 10,000 a week. If delivery were restricted to a pool of 2,000-3,000 reachable designers or engagers, average weekly frequency would be about 3-5. That already sits at the top of the vendor's 3-5/week retargeting cap. So a retargeting-only ad set at the full 50 ILS/day would likely saturate a small engager pool within weeks, and a smaller retargeting slice is more realistic.
- Structure that fits the evidence: 1 campaign, 1 ad set (Advantage+ Audience, Israel), 2-4 ads that test *different ideas* rather than micro-variations, run without edits for at least 7 days, and judged after about 2 weeks (700 ILS). The brief's own stop rule (250 ILS with no relevant conversation, i.e. about 5 days) is a reasonable early-warning check, but given Loomer's small-sample warning it should not be read as a verdict on individual ads.
- With 5-15 relevant conversations a week, A/B comparisons between ads will rarely be statistically meaningful within a month. Only large differences (for example 0 relevant chats against 8) are actionable.
- Raising one ad set toward the 100 ILS/day cap later would double the data rate, but it is still far from 50 events a week.

### Gaps
- Meta's current official learning-phase wording, and whether the lower-threshold experiments reached Israeli accounts, were not verified.
- I found no CTWA-specific CPM or cost-per-conversation data for Israel, and no data on how many of the conversations a broad CTWA ad starts are relevant in a B2B niche.

## 5. Hebrew and Israel specifics

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is close to universal in Israel (98% of users per the Israel Internet Association's 2024 figures, 95% daily), which supports WhatsApp as the conversion channel. Meta CPMs in Israel run well below global medians but swing a lot from month to month. I found no Israeli agency benchmarks for CTWA or B2B lead costs, and no Israel-specific Meta ad-policy issues for Hebrew ads.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp is used by 98% of Israelis and 95% use it daily (Israel Internet Association, 2024; reported by Globes, which ranked WhatsApp Israel's top brand of 2024). The base may be internet users rather than the whole population. — [Globes English](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001484162)
