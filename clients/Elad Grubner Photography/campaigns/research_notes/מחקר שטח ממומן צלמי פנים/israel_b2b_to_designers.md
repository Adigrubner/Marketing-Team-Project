# Israeli businesses that advertise on Meta TO interior designers & architects (B2B) - Ad Library field research

Scope and method (read first): All observations below are VERIFIED in the public Meta Ad Library (country = Israel, active ads only, keyword search, logged out, viewed 15.10.2026) unless marked "Inference". Ad Library sorts results by total impressions (descending) by default, and only the first ~20-30 cards per keyword loaded, so lower-impression ads may be missing. "Started" = the "התחילה לפעול" date on the ad card; durations are counted to 15.10.2026. Format (VIDEO / IMAGE / CAROUSEL) was read from the media element in each card. The Ad Library does not show spend, reach, comments or reactions for ordinary (non-political) Israeli ads. Links: `https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=<ID>` opens the ad (and the advertiser's other active ads). ~70 advertisers with designer/architect-targeted copy were recorded across 31 keyword searches.

## Q1. Which categories advertise to Israeli designers/architects on Meta - and which do not?

### Takeaway
Paying to reach Israeli designers is common and diverse: suppliers/showrooms (furniture, lighting, materials) are the largest group and often run "architects & designers club" ads; next come carpentry/wardrobe companies with designer programs, business coaches/courses for designers, AI-visualization tools/courses and render studios, and tools (software/printing/websites/Instagram). The single longest-running designer-targeted ad found is from a direct competitor, architecture photographer Avi Kabalo (active since 9.10.2024). No active ads were found for reels/video services to designers, staging-for-designers, or "portfolio" services.

### Inferences
- The B2B-to-designer ad market in Israel is mostly suppliers who want to be specified in designers' projects; designers are treated as a valuable, courted audience (clubs, VIP terms, events, coffee at the showroom). Elad's ads compete for attention in a feed where designers already see many "for designers" offers.
- The lack of any reels/video-for-designers advertiser and only one photographer (Avi Kabalo) in these keyword searches suggests the "visual content for designers" niche is thinly contested on Meta (other photographers may advertise with wording my keywords did not catch; this is covered by the separate photographers research file).

### Gaps
- Only the first ~20-30 cards per search loaded (by impressions), so low-spend advertisers may be missing.
- No Hebrew SketchUp/Revit/AutoCAD reseller or Israeli AI-render SaaS brand was found; I did not search brand names individually.
- Home-staging businesses were seen only targeting homeowners or as courses (e.g., Arti college staging course [AL](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1940341890020207)); none targeting designers.

## Q3. Patterns among the longest-running ads - what they show, which pain/promise, what lowers the barrier to message

### Takeaway
The ads that have survived 6-24 months share five traits: (1) the very first words name the profession; (2) they sell a relationship or status, not a product - membership in a "club/team", a personal partner, exposure and credit; (3) they speak to the designer's own professional stakes (her clients, her name on the project, how her work looks), not to aesthetics in general; (4) a concrete low-risk opener - "small message, I'll reply", free first render, free meeting/coffee, sample, checklist, webinar; (5) a one-tap CTA (Messenger/WhatsApp/lead form). The direct competitor's 2-year ad (Avi Kabalo) is the plain version of this: profession call-out, finished-project trigger, a "special benefit", personal intro, Messenger.
